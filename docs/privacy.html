Privacy Policy

Effective August 24, 2026

Lumen is an open-source desktop application for running local AI conversations and Cowork tasks. This policy explains how its optional Google account connection handles data.

Local data

Chats, settings, custom instructions, and memories are stored locally on your device. Model prompts and responses are sent to the model API configured in Lumen. Web searches are sent to the configured search provider only when web search is used.

Google account data

When you choose “Continue with Google,” Lumen requests your basic profile (name, email address, and profile image) to identify the connected account. Lumen also requests access to Google Drive’s application-data folder so it can store and update a private backup of the local Lumen database.

The backup is stored in the hidden Drive application-data folder and is not shared with other users. Lumen does not request access to your ordinary Drive files.

Storage and security

Google access and refresh tokens are encrypted locally with an app-specific, device-bound key. This avoids system keychain prompts. Lumen does not operate a server that receives your Google tokens, chats, or Drive backup.

Sharing and sale

Lumen does not sell personal data. Lumen does not share Google user data with advertisers or data brokers.

Control and deletion

You may disconnect your Google account from Lumen at any time. You can also revoke Lumen’s access from your Google Account permissions. Local chats and memories can be deleted from Lumen’s settings. The Lumen backup can be removed by revoking access and deleting the application data associated with Lumen from Google Drive.
